Who We Are

Bridging Knowledge With The Real World

B-SCIM — Building Scientific Minds — is an educational initiative created to bridge the gap between academic learning and the skills needed in real life and the professional market.

Through practical courses, training sessions and learning opportunities, we help students and fresh graduates build knowledge, confidence and useful skills.
